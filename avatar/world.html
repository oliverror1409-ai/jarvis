JARVIS ISLAND

This is your computer, right now, drawn as an island town. Nothing here is decoration: every district is one part of the machine, every building inside a district is one real program running at this moment — it appears when the program starts, grows with the memory it takes, lights up while it burns CPU, and sinks into the ground when it exits. The little workers are each district's crew: they wander when their programs idle and swing hammers at the exact building that is working hardest. Hover anything for its real numbers.
Windmill sails spin at the node processes' CPU speed · Lighthouse is the graphics card — lamp and beam scale with GPU load · Arcade neon is on only while a game is actually running · Chimney smoke on the Villa means Jarvis is thinking right now · Boats at the pier: one per handful of open browser processes · Rim glow on a district brightens when it is busy · Speech bubbles over the Villa are Jarvis's real replies · Sky, sun, moon and stars follow this computer's actual clock · The WORKING ON RIGHT NOW board lists the heaviest jobs in plain words.
